NCH6300HV, Powering Up Your Nixie Tubes & Magic Eye w/ Ease

Story

(For the latest update, go to the bottom of this page)

What is NCH6300HV?

  • Nixie tubes look unique, pretty, and are fun to play with. Powering them up is not easy though, as they require 170V DC voltage to ignite.
  • We designed the NCH6100HV in 2012, and it has been very popular in the nixie community.
  • 8 years later today, it's time to update it to the brand new NCH6300HV.
  • The NCH6300HV is 19% smaller in footprint, yet almost double the power compared to its precedent. They are pin-to-pin compatible so just drop in and it just works with a minor tweak.
  • The design has been firstly tested in our Omnixie Plus clocks to replace the old model, which has been in mass production since May 2019.
  • Omnixie Plus powered by NCH6300HV design (with ZM-1040 Nixie tube)
  • Omnixie Plus powered by NCH6300HV design (with IN-18 Nixie tube)

Why NCH6300HV?

  • NCH6300HV DC-DC power booster that succeeds its precedent, NCH6100HV, with 19% smaller footprint, higher efficiency and almost double the output current.
  • Its sibling, NCH8200HV, was used by many nixie enthusiasts and professionals. For example, the Puri clock by Dalibor Farny uses our NCH8200HV as power supply in its slim base for years.
  • The most powerful and adaptive 6300HV can power more 10+ the largest nixie tube at low voltage. See how it powered by 5V DC source.
  • And yes, you can do it with Li-ion battery too!
  • NCH6300HV uses Lithium Ion battery to power up IN18 tubes The spec comparison among 6100HV, 6300HV and 8200HV.
  • Feature comparison
  • 12V DC Input can power up 12 the large IN-18 nixie tubes with ease. It can light up ~23 of them (100mA max) if active cooling is properly implemented.
  • 5V DC Input can power up 10 pieces of IN-18 nixie tubes. No problem~
