NeoGoggles Party Glasses

RGB LED glasses with customizable animations and sound reactive mode

Back to Products

What is NeoGoggles?

NeoGoggles is a pair of glasses designed by Omnixie Electronics. It was released in October 2023 at the Bay Area Maker Faire.

NeoGoggles have 2 rings of RGB LEDs featuring numorous animations, controlled by 5 knobs and 1 button on the front. Simply turn the knobs to tune the LED patterns/colors/brightness/speed to find the animation that match your style.

Feature Highlights

  1. Tons of Animations Customized by Knobs/Button to Suite YOUR STYLE
  2. 8 Speeds x 32 Brightness Levels
  3. Hours of fun on a full charge
  4. Rechargeable via USB Type C to Type A cable.
  5. Sound Reactive mode: LED colors will change with your music. (The Pattern knob must be turned all the way counter-clockwise.)

How to Use

Power

  • Slide UP the Power Switch to turn it on. Slide DOWN the Power Switch to turn it off.
  • It takes around 1-2 hours to charge it from 0% to 100%.

Controlling LED Animations

5 Blue Knobs: Section, Color, Brightness, Speed, Pattern

Charging

  • Turn the power switch off for faster charging.
  • Use a USB-A to USB-C cable to charge the product.
  • When charging, the red Charging Indicator LED will turn on, which will turn off after fully charged.
  • It does NOT support USB-C to USB-C cable for charging (yet).

Check Battery Level

  • Press & Hold the "Battery Check" button will show the battery voltage with LEDs.
  • For example, if 3 LEDs are red, 2 LEDs are green and 1 LED is blue, then the battery voltage is 3.21 volt.
  • Battery's voltage is around 4.2V when fully charged, and about 3.7V when out of battery.
